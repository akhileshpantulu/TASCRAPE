TripAdvisor Review Scraper

Runs in your own browser, with a progress tracker for each hotel. No terminal, install or Python needed. Works in Chrome and Edge, and with the Claude Cowork workflow.

1. Add the bookmark

Show your bookmarks bar (Ctrl+Shift+B, or Cmd+Shift+B on Mac). Delete any older TA Reviews bookmark, then drag this green button onto the bar:

TA Reviews

If dragging does not work: right-click the bookmarks bar, choose Add page, name it TA Reviews, and paste the text below into the URL field.

2. Use it by hand

  1. Open one of your hotel pages on tripadvisor.com.
  2. Click the TA Reviews bookmark.
  3. Paste your hotel URLs separated by spaces (or the asset list Claude gave you), or leave the box empty to scrape the hotel you are viewing.
  4. Enter a max number of reviews per hotel, or leave it empty for all.
  5. A tracker panel shows a progress bar for each hotel. Keep the tab open and visible; do not reload it or navigate away. When it finishes, the CSV downloads automatically.

If an orange setup message appears, scroll to the hotel's reviews and click the next-page arrow once.

3. Use it with Claude Cowork

Claude opens your first hotel page with your asset list attached to the link. When it asks, click TA Reviews once. No questions pop up; Claude follows the tracker from there.
