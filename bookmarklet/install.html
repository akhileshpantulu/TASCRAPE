TripAdvisor Review Scraper

Runs in your own browser, so no terminal, install or Python is needed. Works in Chrome and Edge.

1. Add the bookmark

Show your bookmarks bar (Ctrl+Shift+B, or Cmd+Shift+B on Mac), then drag this green button onto it:

TA Reviews

If dragging does not work: right-click the bookmarks bar, choose Add page, name it TA Reviews, and paste the text below into the URL field.

2. Use it

  1. Open tripadvisor.com (any page, or a hotel page).
  2. Click the TA Reviews bookmark.
  3. Paste your hotel URLs separated by spaces, or leave the box empty to scrape the hotel you are viewing.
  4. Answer the two questions (max reviews per hotel, languages).
  5. A green box in the corner shows progress. Keep the tab open. When it finishes, tripadvisor_reviews.csv downloads automatically.

Start with one hotel and a limit of 20 to check it works.
